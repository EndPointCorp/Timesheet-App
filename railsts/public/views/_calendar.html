Shows the status of worked hours during selected months.
Background colors

Days when the required minimum of 8 hours have been worked are marked with green background. The ones shown in red are ones below 8 hours. The current day is marked with an orange background.

Stripes

Some days can be additionally marked with warning stripes. The blue one tells that there's more than one entry created for the same work chart. The pink one tells that there's an entry with no time entered.
